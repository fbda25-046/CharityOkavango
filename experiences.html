Safari Experiences

From silent mokoro journeys to thrilling game drives — every moment is unforgettable

Why Our Experiences Stand Out

We offer small-group, low-impact safaris in private concessions. Every experience is led by expert local guides who were born and raised in the Delta.

Our Signature Experiences

Mokoro Canoe Safaris

Glide silently through the waterways of the Delta in a traditional mokoro canoe. Spot hippos, crocodiles, and birds up close.

  • Duration: 3–4 hours
  • Best time: Early morning or sunset
  • Included: Guide, refreshments, binoculars

Game Drives

Explore the floodplains and woodlands in open 4×4 vehicles. Excellent chance of seeing the Big Five.

  • Duration: 3–6 hours
  • Best time: Morning & late afternoon
  • Included: Expert tracker & guide, snacks, drinks

Luxury Tented Camps

Stay in exclusive, eco-friendly luxury camps with en-suite bathrooms and private decks overlooking the Delta.

  • Duration: 3–7 nights
  • Included: All meals, game drives, mokoro trips

Walking Safaris

Immerse yourself in the bush on foot with armed professional guides. Perfect for those who want a deeper connection with nature.

  • Duration: 2–4 hours
  • Best time: Early morning
