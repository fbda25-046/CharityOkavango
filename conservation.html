Conservation & Community

Tourism that protects the Delta and empowers its people

Our Commitment

At LMK Okavango Adventures, conservation is not a marketing slogan — it is the foundation of our business.

We believe that responsible tourism can be a powerful tool for protecting one of the world’s most important wetlands. Every safari we operate contributes directly to wildlife protection and community development in the Okavango Delta.

Our Key Conservation Initiatives

Anti-Poaching & Wildlife Protection

We fund community game scouts and support anti-poaching patrols in partnership with the Botswana Defence Force and local trusts.

Community Empowerment

Over 60% of our revenue is returned to the Khwai, Mababe, and Sankoyo Community Trusts, funding schools, clinics, and small businesses.

Sustainable Operations

100% solar-powered camps, zero single-use plastics, carbon-neutral safaris, and waste recycling programmes.
