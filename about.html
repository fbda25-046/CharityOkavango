Our Story

Born in the heart of the Delta • Rooted in community • Driven by conservation

Who We Are

LMK Okavango Adventures was founded in 2018 by a group of passionate local families from Maun, Botswana, who grew up on the banks of the Okavango Delta.

Frustrated by mass tourism that often ignored local communities and the fragile ecosystem, our founders decided to create a different kind of safari company — one that puts sustainability, authenticity, and community benefit at the centre of every experience.

Today, LMK operates exclusive private concessions in the Okavango Delta and works in close partnership with the Community-Based Natural Resource Management (CBNRM) trusts of Khwai, Mababe, and Sankoyo. Every safari we run directly supports wildlife conservation and provides income to over 200 local families.

Our Mission

To deliver unforgettable, low-impact luxury safaris that celebrate the wild beauty of the Okavango Delta while empowering local communities and protecting one of the world’s most important wetlands.

Our Vision

To become Botswana’s leading model for responsible eco-tourism — a company that proves tourism can be a powerful force for conservation and community development.

Our Core Values

Sustainability

Carbon-neutral operations, zero single-use plastics, and support for anti-poaching initiatives.

Community First

Profits are shared directly with local trusts — over 60% of revenue stays in the community.

Authentic Luxury

Intimate camps, expert local guides, and personalised experiences in untouched wilderness.
